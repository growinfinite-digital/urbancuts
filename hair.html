Service Overview

The Urban Cuts Hair Experience

Hair is one of the most visible parts of your personal style, and at Urban Cuts every hair service begins with a proper consultation — not a rushed cut. Our stylists take the time to understand your face shape, hair texture, maintenance routine and the look you're going for before any scissors come out.

Whether you're after a sharp everyday haircut, a bold global colour change, or a restorative treatment for hair that's seen too much heat and styling, our team uses premium products and modern technique to deliver results that hold up well after you leave the chair.

Hair services at Urban Cuts include haircuts & styling, hair treatments, global and balayage colour, and finishing styles for everyday wear or special occasions — for men, women and children alike.

Have Questions?

Hair Service FAQs

How often should I get a haircut?

Most clients visit every 4 to 6 weeks to maintain shape, though it depends on your hair type and the style you're keeping.

Do you offer hair colour and treatments for damaged hair?

Yes, our stylists assess hair health during consultation and recommend suitable colour and repair treatments before any chemical service.

Is a patch test required before colouring?

Yes, a patch test is recommended at least 48 hours before any colour service to check for sensitivity.

How long does a typical hair appointment take?

A haircut typically takes 30 to 45 minutes, while colour and treatment services can take 1.5 to 3 hours depending on hair length and service.

Do you offer hair services for children and men?

Yes, Urban Cuts is a unisex family salon offering hair services for men, women and children.
