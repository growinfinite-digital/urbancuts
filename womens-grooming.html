Women's Grooming FAQs

What is the difference between threading and waxing?

Threading removes hair at the follicle using twisted cotton thread and is ideal for precise areas like eyebrows and facial hair. Waxing removes hair over larger areas at once and typically lasts longer between sessions.

What is B Wax?

B Wax refers to bikini-line waxing, a more intimate waxing service performed with care and hygiene as a priority. Speak with our team to discuss the specific coverage you'd like.

What's the difference between Under Arm waxing and Under Arm Peel Off?

Both remove underarm hair; Peel Off uses a peel-off wax formula that's gentler and often preferred for sensitive underarm skin, while regular Under Arm waxing uses strip wax.

What does body polishing actually do?

Body polishing exfoliates dead skin and evens out texture and tone, leaving skin smoother and brighter. It pairs well with waxing for a complete grooming session.

Do you offer saree and dupatta draping for events?

Yes, our team offers Saree Draping and Dupatta Draping services, ideal for weddings, festivals and special occasions — book ahead to secure your slot.

How often should I get threading or waxing done?

Threading is typically touched up every 2 to 3 weeks, while waxing generally lasts 3 to 4 weeks depending on your hair growth cycle.
