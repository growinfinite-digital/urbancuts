Hair Treatment FAQs

What is the difference between Keratin, Hair Botox and Nanoplastia?

All three are smoothening treatments but differ in formulation and finish. Keratin gives the sleekest, straightest result; Hair Botox deep-conditions while adding smoothness and volume; Nanoplastia uses a gentler, formaldehyde-free formula ideal for fine or sensitive hair. Our stylists recommend the right one during consultation.

Why are Keratin, Hair Botox and Nanoplastia priced by hair length?

These treatments use product quantity based on hair length and density, so pricing is split into Upto Neck, Upto Shoulder and Below Shoulder categories to keep it fair and transparent.

How often should I get an anti-hairfall or anti-dandruff treatment?

For active hairfall or dandruff concerns, we typically recommend a session every 2 to 3 weeks, with maintenance sessions monthly once the scalp condition improves.

Is a patch test required before Keratin, Hair Botox or Nanoplastia?

Yes, a patch test is recommended at least 48 hours before any chemical smoothening treatment to check for sensitivity.

What is the difference between Hair Spa Basic and Hair Spa Advance?

Hair Spa Basic is a nourishing everyday spa with steam and massage, while Hair Spa Advance uses a richer formula for deeper hydration, shine and repair, recommended for drier or more damaged hair.
