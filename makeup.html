Have Questions?

Makeup FAQs

How long does party or occasion makeup typically last?

With the right prep and setting products, our party and occasion makeup is designed to stay fresh for 8 to 10 hours, including through photos and dancing.

Can I book a trial session before my event?

Yes, we recommend a trial session ahead of major events like weddings, so we can finalise the look together with time to make changes if needed.

What makeup products do you use?

We use premium, skin-suited cosmetic brands chosen for long wear and a natural finish on camera and in person.

Can I bring reference photos for the look I want?

Yes, reference photos are welcome and genuinely helpful — bring them along or share them when you book so we can prepare accordingly.

How far in advance should I book makeup for a wedding or big event?

We recommend booking at least 1 to 2 weeks ahead for weddings and major events to secure your preferred date and time, and earlier during festival season.

Do you offer hair styling along with makeup?

Yes, hair styling can be booked alongside your makeup appointment — let us know when booking so we can plan the full look together.
