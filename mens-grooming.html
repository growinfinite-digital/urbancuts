Have Questions?

Men's Grooming FAQs

What is the difference between a Shave and a Luxury Shave?

A Shave covers a clean, precise shave with hot towel prep. A Luxury Shave extends this with a longer hot towel ritual, pre-shave oil and a relaxing face massage for a more indulgent experience.

What is Beard Crafting?

Beard Crafting is precision beard sculpting that defines and shapes your beard line for a sharper, more deliberate look, rather than a simple trim.

Is Beard Colour safe and natural-looking?

Yes, our stylists use quality colour products formulated for facial hair and match tone carefully for a natural-looking finish, whether covering greys or enhancing your existing colour.

How often should I get a beard trim?

Most clients book a Beard Trim every 2 to 3 weeks to keep the shape neat, though it depends on how fast your beard grows and the style you're maintaining.

What's included in the Men's Grooming Package?

Our Men's Grooming Package combines a haircut, shave or beard service and grooming essentials into a single appointment, so you leave fully sorted in one visit.

Is shaving suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes, our barbers use hot towel prep and quality products, and can adjust technique for sensitive skin — just mention it before your service.
