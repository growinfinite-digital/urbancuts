Service Overview

Hand & Foot Care, Tiered to How Much Time You Have

Not every visit calls for the same level of pampering. Some days you need a quick, tidy Manicure before a meeting — other days you want the full LUX Pedicure experience with time to properly unwind. That's why our hands and feet menu is built in three simple tiers.

Classic covers the essentials — shaping, cuticle care, a soak and polish. Advanced adds exfoliation and a deeper massage. LUX is our most indulgent option, layering premium products and extended massage for a complete self-care ritual.

Have Questions?

Hands & Feet FAQs

What is the difference between a Classic and an Advanced Manicure or Pedicure?

Classic Manicure and Pedicure cover shaping, cuticle care, a soak and polish. Advanced Mani and Pedi add exfoliation, a deeper massage and richer masks for extra nourishment and relaxation.

What's included in the LUX Manicure and LUX Pedicure?

Our LUX Manicure and LUX Pedicure are our most indulgent tier, layering premium products, extended massage and richer treatments on top of the Advanced service for a complete pampering experience.

How long does a manicure or pedicure typically take?

A Classic Manicure or Pedicure takes about 30 to 40 minutes, Advanced services take 45 to 60 minutes, and LUX services can take up to 75 minutes.

Do you sanitise tools between clients?

Yes, all tools are sanitised or single-use, and every station is cleaned between clients as part of our standard hygiene protocol.

Can I book Mani & Pedi together to save time?

Yes, our Mani & Pedi and Advanced Mani & Pedi combo services let you book both together in a single appointment.

Is manicure and pedicure suitable for sensitive or ticklish feet?

Yes, our technicians adjust pressure and technique to your comfort — let them know at the start of your service.
